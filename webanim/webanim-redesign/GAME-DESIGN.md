# Skyward Express game design

Skyward Express is an arcade drone courier game. Fly a route, brake over the active pad, and release a parcel while accounting for your momentum and the wind. Good drops build a delivery streak. Perfect drops require landing in the center band.

## Career

Start with the Swift X1 and learn a stationary drop and a moving approach in First Drop. Contracts introduce height changes, deadlines, fragile cargo, heavy loads, timed receiving windows, and charging. Earn credits to buy equipment and earn experience to unlock districts. Completing a contract opens the next one. Optional district challenges unlock partway through each district.

| District | Main contracts | Challenges | Focus |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Downtown Deliveries | 8 | 2 | Training, poles and wires, tighter windows |
| Business District | 8 | 2 | High pads, moving crane hooks, narrower passages |
| Harbor & Industrial Zone | 8 | 2 | Coastal wind, low approaches, heavy cargo |
| Neon Heights | 8 | 2 | Night deliveries, rain, restricted zones and security drones |
| Skyward Central | 8 | 2 | High-altitude routes, long sequences and timed windows |

The briefing lists the drop order and star requirements. Completing the route earns one star. The second and third star require the additional listed conditions in order. Replaying can improve a record without replacing a higher rating.

## Arcade and practice

- **Classic Shift** uses the original city, customer, pickup and lives-based rules. Five levels lead to a 2,500-point finish.
- **Endless** starts the arcade engine with an open-ended score target. Camera speed and hazard cadence increase during the run.
- **Time Trial** cycles four stops during a 60-second session.
- **Precision Challenge** gives five attempts to serve three narrow pads with the predictor disabled.
- **Free Flight** cycles three practice pads with unlimited battery and time. The pause menu includes Finish practice.

Difficulty affects campaign pad width, battery-independent time allowance, damage, braking and reward multipliers. Cargo, drone and equipment choices change flight behavior. The player can configure display, input and audio settings through the dispatch menu.
