# Surface Brief: Admin Workstation (Cinema Suite & Darkroom Terminal)

Scope: Operate
Audience: Portfolio owner (photographer, drone pilot, filmmaker)
Primary Target: admin/index.html
Related Targets: admin/analytics.html, admin/settings.html, admin/security.html, admin/backups.html

## Direction contract

THESIS: An obsidian cinema grading suite and darkroom contact sheet that refuses the generic green sidebar SaaS dashboard. Built for visual media first, where photography and aerial film are curated with precision metadata and tactile studio controls.

OWN-WORLD: Deep carbon and obsidian substrate (#090a0f, #0e1117, #151821) with hairline tungsten and amber phosphor accents (#f59e0b, #fbbf24), razor-sharp 1px dividers, monospace camera telemetry (aperture, focal length, shutter, altitude, ISO), film contact strip markers, and a floating frosted command dock.

STORY: The curator enters a darkened production suite. Immediate studio telemetry gives a heartbeat of visitor traffic, site health, and draft changes, then transitions smoothly into high-density contact sheets and an integrated slide-over inspector for immediate editing without workflow interruptions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sleek top HUD ribbon displays live telemetry (sync beacon, visitors, draft status, cloud deploy). Below it, a full-bleed contact sheet stage organizes active shoots and galleries. A floating bottom command dock anchors mode switching and quick publishing. Clicking any asset draws out the right-hand Darkroom Inspector.

FORM: Cinema Suite & Darkroom Terminal with Mission Control Telemetry HUD and Slide-over Precision Inspector, position 7 on the grounded candidate list, seed key 3aeae9b9.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
