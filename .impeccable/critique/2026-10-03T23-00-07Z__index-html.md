#### Overall Impression
A visually compelling, high-craft photography portfolio with authentic editorial work and clean typography, undermined primarily by the misplaced presence of an automated AI chat widget and minor heading/contrast oversights that break accessibility standards.

#### What's Working
1. **Compelling Editorial Imagery & Authenticity**: Real local projects (The La Salle Yearbook, Tigris, Cadiz City aerials) with custom-tailored gear and workflow credits create genuine professional trust.
2. **Accessible Lightbox with Keyboard Control**: Full keyboard support (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Escape`) with clean numeric counters (`1 / 18`) and dedicated mobile details toggles.
3. **Resilient Performance & Fallback Strategy**: Responsive WebP source sets across multiple breakpoints (800w, 1200w, 1600w, 2048w) backed by solid `<noscript>` navigation and `onerror` image fallbacks.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Intrusive AI Chatbot Widget Clutters Visual Portfolio**
  - **Why it matters**: A floating "DeepSeek V4 Flash" chatbot widget in the bottom-right corner introduces unwanted visual clutter, distracts from the visual artwork, and triggers automated AI-slop penalties (`pulsing-dot`, `gradient-text`). Fine-art photography portfolios demand calm, focused immersion.
  - **Fix**: Remove the AI chat widget from `portfolio.js` and `portfolio.css`, or restrict it to an explicitly separate lab/experiments surface.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Broken Document Heading Hierarchy & Stray Markup**
  - **Why it matters**: `index.html` skips heading ranks (`h1` "Framing Stories." jumps to `h3` "Drone Shots"; `h2` "The Photographer." jumps to `h4` "Camera Kit"). Screen readers and search indexers rely on sequential hierarchy. Furthermore, `drone-shots.html` has an orphaned `</div>` at line 91 that risks parser misbehavior.
  - **Fix**: Promote category card titles to `h2` or add a semantic `h2` section heading; promote kit headers to `h3`; remove the stray `</div>` tag in `drone-shots.html`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Undersized Functional UI Badges & Low-Contrast Text Overlays**
  - **Why it matters**: The `text-[10px]` category badges ("Aerial Views", "Street & Editorial", etc.) violate the 11px accessibility threshold. Additionally, gallery card hover overlays rely entirely on CSS text-shadow over raw photos without a gradient scrim; over high-key shots (bright sky, sand), white text becomes illegible.
  - **Fix**: Elevate badge font size to `text-xs` (12px), and introduce a subtle dark gradient scrim (`from-black/70 via-black/30 to-transparent`) behind card descriptions.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] Inefficient YouTube Hover Previews in Video Projects**
  - **Why it matters**: In `video-projects.html`, hovering any video card for 400ms spawns an active YouTube iframe with `autoplay=1&mute=1`. Mousing over multiple cards triggers network thrashing, high RAM usage, and sluggish interactions.
  - **Fix**: Replace dynamic iframe injection with lightweight WebP animated previews or static thumbnail posters, loading the YouTube player only upon explicit user click/tap into the lightbox.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`
