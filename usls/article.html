USLS Nursing places 10th nationwide, with two topnotchers.

29 September 2026Academic achievement

Image: University of St. La Salle.

The University of St. La Salle's nursing program was recognized among the country's top performers in the 2026 nursing licensure examination. USLS placed 10th nationwide in the group of schools with at least 100 examinees and an 85 percent or higher passing rate.

Allanah Beatriz Tubal ranked fifth among examinees, while Johanna Maisie San Jose placed 10th. The university reported 186 successful examinees, a 98.41 percent overall passing rate, and a 99.46 percent rate for first-time takers.

These results reflect the effort of students, faculty, and the wider Lasallian community. Read the university's full report for the complete announcement and acknowledgements.
