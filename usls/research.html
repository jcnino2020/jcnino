Inquiry in service of others.

Research at USLS connects scholarship, ethical practice, publication, and engagement with communities.

The Center for Research and Engagement coordinates the university's research work and connects inquiry with community needs.

From question to contribution

Faculty and student researchers can work through the university's structures for planning, review, sharing results, and building partnerships.

Research development

Research begins with a relevant question, a clear proposal, and a sound method. CRE supports researchers as they develop work within university priorities and disciplinary standards.

Ethical practice

Projects involving people or communities require careful review of consent, privacy, and participant welfare. Use the current CRE guidance for submission and review procedures.

Publication and knowledge sharing

USLS supports dissemination through research publications, presentations, and other avenues that make findings useful beyond the campus.

Engagement and partnerships

Research and extension connect the university with schools, organizations, and communities to address practical challenges.

Research structures and current documents: official Center for Research and Engagement. Forms and policies can change.
