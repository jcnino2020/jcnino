# University of St. La Salle Website Concept

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Prospective students and applicants are the primary audience, confirmed by the user. Current students, families, and researchers are secondary audiences served through clear resource paths.

## Product Purpose
An independent redesign concept for the University of St. La Salle in Bacolod City. It should read with the clarity and usefulness of a university website, while routing real application, account, and information tasks to verified official USLS destinations.

## Operating Context
This is a static six-page subsite in a personal portfolio: home, academics, admissions, research, campus life, and one news story. It does not own a university account system, admissions records, or a live news feed. Institutional information was checked against usls.edu.ph on 9 October 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Keep all six existing routes and make their navigation consistent and functional.
- Present verified university structure: seven colleges, professional schools, basic education, Liceo De La Salle, and graduate studies.
- Route applications to the official USLS admissions pages and AIMS portal. Do not create a fake application form or link to unrelated services.
- Use current official news and quality information with source links and dates; do not imply that static content updates automatically.
- Keep an unobtrusive but clear disclosure that this is an independent redesign concept and link back to the official website.
- Preserve the university name, Bacolod location, and its official line “Live Your Passion, Create Better Futures.”

## Brand Commitments
The user wants the experience to read like a university site, not a portfolio case study. The institution's actual green identity and official logo are relevant; the original dark technology-dashboard styling is not binding. The hero may use the portfolio owner's own photograph `../images/Drone Shots/Drone-002.JPG`, which visibly depicts the USLS campus.

## Product Principles
- Help an applicant choose the right official path quickly.
- Treat university facts as sourced and time-sensitive.
- Show real campus and student work rather than generic stock imagery.
- Keep every visible action functional and honest about where it goes.
