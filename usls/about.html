La Salle Bacolod.

A university shaped by education, faith, service, and a commitment to better futures for Negros and beyond.

Founded in 1952, the University of St. La Salle grew from a small school in Bacolod into a university serving learners from basic education through professional study.

The school began with 175 boys in preparatory through Grade 5 classes. It expanded into higher education in the following decades and was granted university status in 1988. Today, USLS is part of the worldwide Lasallian education network.

Its campus on La Salle Avenue brings together academic life, student organizations, research, arts, sports, and community engagement.

Milestones

  1. 1952 · A new school in Bacolod

    Three De La Salle Brothers established La Salle-Bacolod on land offered for a school in the city.

  2. 1960s · Higher education expands

    The school grew from its early basic education offering into college-level study.

  3. 1988 · University status

    The institution became the University of St. La Salle on 5 July 1988.
