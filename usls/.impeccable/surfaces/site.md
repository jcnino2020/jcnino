# USLS: The Admissions Prospectus

Mode: Persuade on the home and admissions routes; Read on academics, research, campus life, and news. Code-first, based on the user's earlier direct-build preference; no shared build-path setting is recorded.

## World
An institutional admissions prospectus rather than a dashboard: full-color campus photography, La Salle green as the anchor, warm white editorial pages, restrained gold wayfinding marks, and a disciplined typographic grid. The official university name and motto remain central. The masthead behaves like a real university navigation system, with applicant, student, and research destinations clearly separated. Section rules and index rows carry information instead of nested cards.

## First viewport
Home: the authentic USLS campus aerial is full bleed with “University of St. La Salle” as the H1, the official “Live Your Passion, Create Better Futures” line, and two immediate applicant actions. The next pathway section peeks into the viewport on mobile and desktop. No generic stock students, fabricated deadline badge, or fake search bar.

## Visitor path
Applicant sees the campus and chooses Academics or Admissions. Admissions splits undergraduate, basic education, and professional schools, with official current links and a high-level checklist. Academics lists the seven verified colleges and additional levels. Research routes to the official Center for Research and Engagement. Campus Life routes to actual student services and media. News presents one verified story summary and an original-source link.

## Signature interaction
The applicant pathway selector on the home page changes one clearly bounded information area among College, Basic Education, and Professional Schools, preserving keyboard semantics and focus. The mobile navigation opens as a predictable disclosure. All external official actions reveal their destination through text or accessible labels.

## Cross-surface reach
The same green masthead, rule system, editorial headline and action grammar cover both aspirational home content and dense college/requirements lists. Interior pages use compact title bands and information rows rather than repeating full hero sections.

## Honest risk
This is a static portfolio concept and may lag the official site after 9 October 2026. Official admissions, scholarship, academic, account, and news links are the final authority. Keep a footer disclosure and source links; do not mimic an authenticated service.

## Quality bar
The first screen should read as a credible university website within seconds: real place, exact institution, relevant next action. Mobile navigation and tables must not overflow. Visual contrast, image crops, and legible source attribution matter more than decorative animation.

## Challengers considered
Oscilloscope, algorave, and kiln-shelf worlds lose both university identification and applicant clarity; retain their precise state changes, responsiveness, and material honesty as discipline, not motifs. A zoo guide map supports wayfinding but its playful lobes misread as a school campaign; keep navigation legibility. A design annual's plate section offers useful photo scale and strong rules but is more portfolio-like than admissions-like; keep its typography discipline. An akari workshop conveys welcome but obscures program structure; keep warmth without paper-glow decoration.
