# HydroAlert Direction Contract

## THESIS
A hydrological monitoring desk makes the link between upstream observation and downstream awareness visible through calibrated traces, a station roster, and a deliberately simulated event sequence.

## OWN-WORLD
Bright neutral work surface, forest-green structural header, readable Public Sans, blue water traces, amber rising states and red high-water states. Flat ruled sections, compact controls, four-pixel corners. No dark glass, photograph hero, gradient headings, phone frames, fake maps or decorative dashboard metrics.

## STORY
Monitoring opens directly at the usable station/scenario workspace. Community opens with an explicit example advisory and station context, followed by official resources and a preparedness checklist. Field reporting opens with an observation form and local report history. Prototype labeling persists across all three views.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Compact brand and three-route navigation over a plainly labeled simulated-data banner. A 32px route heading, device-clock readout and run/reset actions lead to a selected-station roster and large water-level trace. No marketing hero. At narrow widths, navigation wraps, the roster becomes two columns and trace follows at legible width. Community and field views prioritize their own task rather than duplicating the desktop monitor.
