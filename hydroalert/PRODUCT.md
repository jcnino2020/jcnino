# HydroAlert

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Inferred from the existing prototype: monitoring officers, field observers, and residents exploring a community river-warning concept in Negros. No production deployment or verified operational audience is established.

## Product Purpose
A three-view prototype connecting upstream observations, a monitoring scenario, and downstream community awareness.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve /hydroalert/, /hydroalert/mobile/, and /hydroalert/resident.html. Retain replayable upstream/downstream simulation, station detail, community alert preview, preparedness checklist, observation reporting, photo attachment, and report history.
The original supplied prototype had no sensor feed, flood prediction model, dispatch endpoint, weather API, GPS integration, or remote report synchronization. The redesigned field view supports optional permission-based device location and IndexedDB storage of local reports and photos; it does not verify station location or transmit reports. Do not claim live monitoring, confirmed safety, actual rescue dispatch, guaranteed warning lead time, or delivery to an authority.
Illustrative levels and timings must stay visibly labeled. Local reports may be stored in this browser but must never be represented as sent to officials. The device clock is a clock, not an observation freshness indicator. No geographic accuracy may be implied by the schematic.

## Brand Commitments
User confirmed a full HydroAlert redesign, following independent domain-appropriate redesigns of GNET and Y2K38. Preserve HydroAlert and Negros context; do not imitate the photography portfolio. Continue the user's code-first workflow.

## Evidence on Hand
Three existing static HTML prototypes and two screenshots. Original locations include Minoyan/Murcia, Mandalagan/Bacolod, and Don Salvador. Data is synthetic, and the originally stated two-hour prediction is not validated.

## Product Principles
Make the prototype boundary impossible to miss. Distinguish device time from measured data. Explain alert state through text as well as color. Preserve manual control of simulated scenarios. Provide honest local persistence and recoverable errors.
