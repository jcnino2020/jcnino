Why does 1901 appear?

The final positive value has a leading zero followed by 31 ones. Adding one produces a leading one followed by 31 zeros. In signed two's-complement form, that pattern represents -2,147,483,648.

Interpreted as seconds before the epoch, it becomes 1901-12-13 20:45:52 UTC.

The limits of this model

This experiment explicitly keeps the low 32 bits. Actual signed overflow in languages such as C can have undefined behavior; date APIs may instead return errors.

Requested seconds are exact signed 64-bit integers. The date display is limited to the JavaScript calendar range. Leap seconds are not modeled.
