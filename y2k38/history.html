When counters
run out of room.

Related failures show why finite representations matter. These are not all instances of the Unix year 2038 boundary.

Evidence / context
2013

Deep Impact

NASA's lessons-learned record describes a clock-time conversion overflow that disabled the spacecraft. A timing calculation can determine whether a spacecraft can orient itself and communicate.

NASA Lessons Learned: Deep Impact
Time conversion overflowThe confirmed lesson is broader than Unix time: test the full conversion path and its numeric limits.
2014

YouTube & Gangnam Style

Google documented the video reaching two billion views. That puts a view counter close to the signed 32-bit maximum of 2,147,483,647. The widely repeated “broke YouTube” story is not evidence of a Unix timestamp failure.

Google's two-billion-view milestone
Capacity example, not a clock failureViews and seconds share the same integer limit only when stored in the same representation. This page does not claim a verified negative-count crash.
2015

Boeing 787 generator controls

An FAA directive described a software counter overflow after 248 days of continuous power. Generator control units could enter failsafe mode together, creating a risk of losing all AC electrical power.

FAA: 2015 airworthiness directive
Elapsed-time counter overflowA different counter and timescale from Unix seconds. The 2015 directive required repetitive electrical power deactivation.

The shared lesson

A counter's unit, width, signedness, and conversion rules determine its useful lifetime. A device's physical lifespan can exceed the assumptions built into its software.
