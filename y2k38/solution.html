Platform support isn't the whole fix.

GNU's guidance supports wider timestamps through _TIME_BITS=64 on relevant glibc builds. Build configuration and compatibility with dependent libraries matter.

Microsoft documents separate 32-bit and 64-bit time APIs and discourages _USE_32BIT_TIME_T. A wider integer does not mean that every date-conversion function accepts its entire arithmetic range.
